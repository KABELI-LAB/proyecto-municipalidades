Centered dialog for confirmations and short decisions; one clear primary action.

```jsx
<Modal title="¿Enviar solicitud?" footer={<Button>Sí, enviar</Button>}>Revisaremos tus documentos en 2 días hábiles.</Modal>
```

See Modal.d.ts for all props. Styling lives in components/hds.css (hds- classes) and resolves to tokens in tokens/*.css.
