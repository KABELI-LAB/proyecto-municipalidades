Benefit/subsidy card: who it is for, what you need, deadline, and Postular.

```jsx
<BeneficioCard title="Subsidio de agua potable" audience="Hogares del 40% más vulnerable según RSH" deadline="30 de octubre" daysLeft={5} />
```

See BeneficioCard.d.ts for all props. Styling lives in components/hds.css (hds- classes) and resolves to tokens in tokens/*.css.
