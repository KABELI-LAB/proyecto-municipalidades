Emergency communication: nivel (SENAPRED-aligned), zona, mensaje, hora, acción. Overrides all other content.

```jsx
<EmergencyAlert level="roja" title="Evacuación preventiva" zone="Ribera del Mataquito, sector Los Coipos" message="Por crecida del río, dirígete a la Escuela de Hualañé." time="14:35" />
```

See EmergencyAlert.d.ts for all props. Styling lives in components/hds.css (hds- classes) and resolves to tokens in tokens/*.css.
