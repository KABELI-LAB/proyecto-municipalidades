The river-band shape primitive (from the shield waves) for backgrounds, separators and photo masks.

```jsx
<div style={{height:160}}><Cauce colors={['var(--blue-500)','var(--green-500)','var(--yellow-400)']} /></div>
```

See Cauce.d.ts for all props. Styling lives in components/hds.css (hds- classes) and resolves to tokens in tokens/*.css.
