Part-to-whole for ≤5 categories, with center total and a direct legend.

```jsx
<DonutChart centerLabel="M$ 8.240" data={[{label:'Salud',value:38},{label:'Educación',value:34}]} />
```

See DonutChart.d.ts for all props. Styling lives in components/hds.css (hds- classes) and resolves to tokens in tokens/*.css.
