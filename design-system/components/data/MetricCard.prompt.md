KPI tile: label, mono value, unit, delta vs. previous period, optional progress.

```jsx
<MetricCard label="Trámites en línea" value="1.284" delta="+18%" caption="vs. septiembre" />
```

See MetricCard.d.ts for all props. Styling lives in components/hds.css (hds- classes) and resolves to tokens in tokens/*.css.
